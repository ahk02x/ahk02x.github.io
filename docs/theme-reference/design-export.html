Why I build

Something of my own.

At work, I help keep systems running that people depend on. At home, I want to build things I can keep, understand and change myself. That's why there's a lab in my office, why I save my project history, and why I keep coming back to local software.

One early attempt was a little assistant I called Jarvis. In a Windows terminal, I taught it to say hello. Then I tried to get it to remember things. The long-term ambition is a personal assistant running on hardware I control, with a memory I can take with me.
