How this is made

A Raspberry Pi in the footwell logs 53 engine-computer channels five times a second on every drive and pushes the data home. A Sunday job compares the week to the week before and to a four-week baseline. The verdict rules are written down: oil at or above 130 °C or transmission fluid at or above 110 °C flags; a crank below 9.6 V flags; misfires at 1.5× the baseline flag and 1.25× is a watch; any Pi boot storm is a watch. Nothing here is a guess, and nothing here is live: this is a static snapshot by design. More in the garage.
