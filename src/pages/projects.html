{{glyph:crucix}}

Crucix — local-model lab

A side project for learning where a small open model running on my own GPU genuinely helps and where it only looks like it does. A 7B model reads a news sweep and takes a position on dated market questions; a no-drift model prices the same questions, and both are scored on the same outcomes, on paper only. 99 days, 7,538 model calls, roughly 11 GPU-hours. No edge so far — all of the apparent gain traces to a single instrument the news feed could only ever read one way, so the gate stays shut and that finding stands. Five hypotheses killed before any of them cost anything; a lab that can refuse its own idea is the entire point.

running
