ahkira · Coming soon

What's next

None of this is built yet. It ships one piece at a time, and only after it passes the same attack tests.

Next up

  • Public launch. You type your own question and ahkira answers. Unlisted for a week first.
  • A health check that naps it. If anything misbehaves, ahkira goes to sleep on its own until I look.
  • Nightly attack re-runs. The whole lab suite runs every night against the live code.
  • Firmer limits. Rate limits that can't be overrun, even by a lot of visitors at once.

Later

  • A better phone layout. Fewer question chips, and the text box easier to reach.
  • Visitor stats. Totals only, with no per-person tracking.
  • A glow for the mascot. It shows when I'm around, and the house never has to accept a connection to do it.
  • A private ahkira. My own assistant at home, kept completely separate from the public one.

I'll post results as each one lands. Including what broke.
