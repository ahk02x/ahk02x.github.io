What I'm building

A real conversation: you type any question about my work, and ahkira answers from the pages on this site. The public launch follows a private run and checks of the deployed setup.

How it works

  1. Your question goes to a small server at the edge of the internet, never to my home network. Nothing in my house is reachable from this page.
  2. An open-source AI model answers using only the public pages on this site and the answers above. It has no tools, no memory between chats, and nothing private to give away.
  3. Every reply is checked before you see it. If anything looks wrong, you get a safe stock reply instead.

How it stays safe

  • A bot check and rate limits, to limit abuse and usage.
  • Short questions, short answers, and daily usage caps. When the budget runs out, ahkira naps until tomorrow.
  • A hidden tripwire that catches attempts to pull out its instructions.
  • An off switch, and a health check every 15 minutes that puts ahkira to sleep on its own if the model stops answering.
  • Chats are saved so ahkira can get better, and may be used to train it. Emails and phone numbers are blanked out before anything is saved, and saved chats never go back into what ahkira reads when it answers.

How it gets tested

  1. Hundreds of scripted attacks in a lab: jailbreaks, tricks to extract its instructions, attempts to make it leak private details (with fake secrets planted as bait), and off-topic or abusive requests.
  2. Three frontier AI models take turns as the attacker, trying to trick it. Every trick that works becomes a permanent test.
  3. A private run on the real setup, then a quiet launch.
  4. Planned for after launch: the attacks re-run every night, and a failure puts ahkira to sleep until I look.
