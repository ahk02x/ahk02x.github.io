I taught myself my car's diagnostic cable.

The tuning cable only talked to a Windows app, so I worked out the framing and checksum byte by byte and built a read-only logger. What that bought me was the channel map; the car logs over its own internal bus now, with no vendor hardware in the chain. It showed a pattern I hadn't seen before: nearly 40% of misfire counts land in the second after an upshift.
