Infra

The hardware I run

Most of it is second-hand or rescued. All of it is mine to break.

Network

  • blackwall: a Dell Vostro 460 (i5-2400, 8 GB) turned into an OPNsense firewall. It does DNS, DHCP and traffic accounting for the house. It runs on a Corsair RM650x after the original power supply died.
  • Switching: an Aruba 1930 managed switch, and a spare router working as a WiFi access point.

Servers

  • jarvis: Ryzen 5 5600GE, 32 GB DDR4, 1 TB NVMe on ZFS, RTX 2070 SUPER. Proxmox VE with Home Assistant and nine containers: a local language model, speech-to-text, Git hosting, a password manager, a file share and the car's data pipeline. About 111 W at idle.
  • sentry: an Intel N5105 mini PC (8 GB) that runs the room: the wall display, the Stream Deck and the lights.
  • Storage: three 4 TB IronWolf drives in RAIDZ1, about 6.9 TB usable.

Desk and wall

  • PHOSPHOR: a Dell U2719D hung in portrait. It shows the health of everything and latches alarms until I clear them. How it works.
  • Stream Deck MK.2: fifteen keys for the room, the lights and the PC's power.
  • AMIR-PC: Ryzen 9 3900X, RTX 3080 12 GB, 32 GB, Windows 11.
  • MacBook Pro 14: M3, 16 GB. Where everything gets written.

Car

  • Audi SQ5: a Raspberry Pi 4 in a 3D-printed case and a CANable 2.0 Pro on the diagnostic bus. It logs every drive and sends it home to the home server. Inside VIP.

Around the house

  • An LG C2 OLED, Google Home speakers and Govee lights, all driven through Home Assistant.
