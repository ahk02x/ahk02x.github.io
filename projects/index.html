VIP · Vehicle Intelligence Platform

Not more data, more understanding. Built on a 2025 Audi SQ5, logged five times a second through a reverse-engineered cable and a Pi in the footwell, and the app that turns those drives into understanding. Build log, a weekly health card the car writes about itself, and the tools built for it.

IoT

House devices driven by my own code, no vendor cloud: strip lights, a TV, a retired phone as a camera. One shape every time: learn what the device speaks, prove it with a tiny script, wrap it in an HTTP API, then a phone page.

Coach Claude

My gym coach. Every set gets logged against a weekly plan, a progress page tracks the lifts, and the day's notification comes with a line for that body part. Leg day gets the worst of it.

running

flowtrace

Walks every conversation in a packet capture through the DNS, TCP, TLS and application stages and shows where it stalled. Silence and refusal are different failures, and where the silence starts names the guilty layer.

built

macwatch

An hourly snapshot of what malware would touch on the Mac, diffed against the last, plus a five-minute check for commands wedged on a prompt. Born after an install sat on a password box for ten hours. A log is not an alert; the wall is.

running

netdash

A live dashboard for the home firewall: throughput, per-device rates, who is talking to what, what got blocked, and CPU temperature read straight from the box. It runs on the firewall itself.

running

Inbox hub

WhatsApp and Telegram land in one chat, each message sorted into a tier from urgent to mute, with a line saying why it reached me and buttons to re-tier the sender. Voice notes are transcribed locally. Pairing is the last step.
